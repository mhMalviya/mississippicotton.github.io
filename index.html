Single-Ply vs. 1000 TC Bedsheets

Thread count is the number of threads woven into one square inch of fabric. Many 1000 TC bedsheets inflate it by twisting thin yarns together.

Bedsheets FAQ

Are Egyptian cotton bedsheets worth it?

Yes. Long-staple Egyptian cotton is smoother, stronger and more breathable than regular cotton, and it gets softer with every wash.

Are 1000 TC bedsheets or 1000 thread count sheets better?

Not always. Many 1000 TC bedsheets reach that number with multi-ply yarns, which can feel heavy and pill. Fiber quality matters more than thread count.

Why choose single-ply over 1000 TC sheets?

Single-ply long-staple cotton gives the silky, hotel-quality feel of 1000 TC bedsheets with better breathability, durability and price.

What is in the bed sheet set?

Our 4-piece Egyptian cotton bed sheet set includes a flat sheet, a 15” deep-pocket fitted sheet and pillowcases in sateen weave.

Can I buy fitted sheets and pillowcases separately?

Yes. Egyptian cotton fitted sheets, sateen pillowcases and 600 TC percale fitted sheets are all sold individually.

What sizes do your Egyptian cotton bed sheets come in?

Our bed sheets and sheet sets come in sizes from Twin XL to Queen and King, including King pillowcases. See Amazon for current sizes and colors.

How do I care for cotton bedsheets?

Machine wash cold or warm on gentle, skip bleach and tumble dry low.
